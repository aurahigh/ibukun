WHO WE ARE

Design with

FORMA is an independent design studio exploring the relationship between people and the spaces around them.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Spaces should
support life.

We believe thoughtful design starts with listening. Every project begins by understanding the people, rhythms and ambitions that make a space meaningful.

Our work balances clean lines with tactile materials, natural light with practical function, and timeless foundations with personal expression.
